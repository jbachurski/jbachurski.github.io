Jakub Bachurski

Hello there! I'm Jakub Bachurski. I go by Kuba.
I have graduated my BA and MEng in Computer Science at the University of Cambridge in 2025, after matriculating in 2021 at Trinity College. I ran the Competitive Programming Society in 2022–2025.
I'm now a Software Engineer in London. I work at Jane Street in the OCaml Language team, where I hack on OxCaml's type checker and compiler.

I'm broadly interested in research on functional programming languages [scholar] [orcid]. Particularly:
  • Language and type system design.
  • Type inference – especially with let-generalisation, structural types, subtyping, GADTs, and and constraint-based approaches.
  • Data-parallel programming – especially array/tensor programs on GPUs.

I like computer science. These things are pretty cool, in no particular order:
  • ray marching,
  • Datalog,
  • abstract algebra. Among more concrete algebras, I like:
    • complex numbers,
    • regular expressions,
  • theory and interpretability of deep learning,
  • data structures and algorithms,
  • game development.
