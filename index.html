Jakub Bachurski

Hello there! I'm Jakub Bachurski. I go by Kuba. [github] [linkedin]

I'm now at Jane Street in the OCaml Language team, where I hack on OxCaml's type checker and compiler.
I graduated my BA and MEng in Computer Science at the University of Cambridge in 2025. I had ran the Competitive Programming Society since 2022.

I'm broadly interested in research on functional programming [scholar] [orcid]. Particularly:
  • Language and type system design.
  • Type inference – especially with let-generalisation, GADTs, structural types, subtyping, or constraint-based approaches.
  • Data-parallel programming – especially array/tensor programs on GPUs.

I like computer science – and sometimes even computers. These things are pretty cool:
  • ray marching,
  • Datalog,
  • abstract algebra. Among more concrete algebras, I like:
    • complex numbers,
    • regular expressions,
  • theory and interpretability of deep learning,
  • data structures and algorithms,
  • computer game design & development.
